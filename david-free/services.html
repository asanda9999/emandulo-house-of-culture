01 / DRESS THE OCCASION

Imvunulo & Beadwork

Traditional attire and accessories prepared with cultural accuracy, so the wearer arrives already belonging to the moment.

Emandulo sources, styles and completes imvunulo for ceremonies, celebrations and creative briefs. Pieces are chosen for meaning as much as for presence: colour, form and beadwork that honour the family, the rite and the setting.

Where this is used

  • Traditional weddings, umembeso, umabo and related rites
  • Family ceremonies, heritage days and cultural celebrations
  • Custom beadwork and finishing for a defined brief
  • Styling direction so the full look reads as one language

02 / SHAPE THE MOMENT

Cultural Events

Cultural planning and coordination for occasions that must feel true — not staged, not generic, not borrowed.

We help shape the flow of a gathering: what happens, in what order, and how people are received. The work sits between protocol and hospitality, so the day holds together from arrival to close.

Where this is used

  • Private cultural ceremonies and family occasions
  • Corporate and institutional heritage programmes
  • Public cultural moments that need a clear structure
  • Coordination with attire, hosting and production partners

03 / HOLD THE ROOM

MC & Programme Direction

Confident programme direction for cultural, corporate and private events — presence that keeps the room oriented.

Hosting here is not filler. It is timing, tone and respect for the people being honoured. We guide the programme so speakers, rites and transitions land cleanly, and so guests always know where they are in the day.

Where this is used

  • Traditional and contemporary ceremonies
  • Corporate functions with a cultural brief
  • Private celebrations that need a steady voice
  • Programme design, cueing and on-the-day direction

05 / CULTURE ON SCREEN

Production Wardrobe

Cultural wardrobe and styling for screen, stage and editorial production — prepared for camera, not only for the room.

Production work demands more than a beautiful garment. It needs accuracy, continuity and pieces that read under light. We support wardrobe teams and directors who want South African cultural dress treated as knowledge, not as decoration.

Where this is used

  • Film, television and commercial production
  • Stage, campaign and editorial styling
  • Cultural consultancy on dress, colour and protocol
  • On-set support for fittings, look and continuity
